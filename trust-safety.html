Our Commitment to Trust & Safety

Trust is not a feature — it is a commitment that guides every decision we make. We are committed to:

  • Treating every member with respect.
  • Encouraging honest, transparent communication.
  • Protecting user accounts and information.
  • Supporting fair project workflows for clients and contributors alike.
  • Continuously improving platform security.
  • Building long-term relationships based on transparency.

Reporting & Appeals

If you encounter behavior that appears to violate Harmonia's policies — fraud, harassment, impersonation, or abuse of platform features — we encourage you to report it through our official support channels. Every report is reviewed carefully and fairly.

If you believe an account action or platform decision was made in error, you may contact our support team to request a review. We are committed to treating every appeal respectfully and objectively.

Responsible Use of Technology

Automated tools help us improve efficiency, detect unusual activity, and assist with quality assurance — but important platform decisions also involve human review, particularly when fairness and context matter. We believe the best outcomes come from combining technology with thoughtful human oversight.
