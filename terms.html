2. About Harmonia

Harmonia is a Business-to-Business (B2B) AI workforce platform. We connect businesses, enterprises, and researchers (Clients) with a global network of independent contributors (Contributors or Workers).

Our core services involve facilitating AI data annotation, AI model evaluation, data collection, and human feedback tasks. Clients require high-quality human-generated data to train artificial intelligence models, and Contributors provide this labor in exchange for financial compensation.

Harmonia provides the software infrastructure, quality assurance tools, and payment facilitation necessary to connect these two parties. We do not guarantee specific business outcomes for Clients, nor do we guarantee continuous work availability for Contributors.

4. Platform Services

Harmonia offers a variety of digital work opportunities related to the training and evaluation of artificial intelligence. Depending on client demand, the Platform may offer tasks including, but not limited to:

  • AI Data Annotation (Image, Video, Audio, Text)
  • Large Language Model (LLM) Evaluation
  • AI Prompt Evaluation and Generation
  • Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)

  • Micro-Tasks and Challenge Codes

5. Contributor Responsibilities

Contributors participate on the Platform as independent contractors. To maintain access to the Platform and ensure high-quality data for our Clients, Contributors must adhere to the following responsibilities:

  • Follow Instructions: Carefully read and strictly follow the specific guidelines provided for each AI task or project.
  • Submit Original Work: All submissions must be the genuine, unassisted work of the logged-in Contributor.
  • No Cheating or Automation: The use of automated scripts, bots, or artificial intelligence (e.g., ChatGPT) to complete tasks is strictly prohibited unless explicitly authorized for a specific project.
  • Quality Standards: Submissions must meet the quality standards outlined by the Client and Harmonia. Repeated low-quality submissions may result in account suspension.
  • Respect Confidentiality: Contributors must not share, leak, or publish any project data encountered during tasks.

6. Client Responsibilities

Clients use the Platform to source human intelligence for their AI projects. To ensure a fair and legal ecosystem, Clients must adhere to the following:

  • Provide Legal Datasets: Clients must ensure that any data uploaded to the Platform is legal and does not contain malicious, harmful, or explicit content without proper context and warning.
  • Data Ownership: Clients must own the data they upload or possess the legal rights and licenses to use and distribute it for annotation purposes.
  • Respect Privacy Laws: Clients must ensure their datasets comply with applicable privacy laws, including GDPR and CCPA, and that any personal data has been properly anonymized or consented to.
  • No Copyright Infringement: Clients must not upload copyrighted content without the explicit permission of the copyright holder.

  • Payment Schedules: Payout processing times may vary based on the payment provider (PayPal or Crypto) and administrative review schedules.
