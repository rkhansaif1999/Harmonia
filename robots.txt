# Logged-in-only dashboards — kept out of search results via each page's
# own <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> tag rather than a robots.txt
# Disallow. Disallowing here would stop crawlers from ever fetching the
# page at all, which also blocks legitimate third-party verification bots
# (e.g. ad-network crawlers that confirm an ad tag is really present on
